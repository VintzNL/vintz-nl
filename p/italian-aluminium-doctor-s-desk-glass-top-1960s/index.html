Some desks are made to impress. This one was made to work. It comes from a doctor's practice, and that purpose still shows in every line: an Italian aluminium desk from the 1960s with two black pedestals, six drawers and nothing that does not need to be there.

The frame does the talking. Bent aluminium tube forms the legs, the sled shaped runners and the support under the top, so the whole desk seems to rest on a few calm silver lines. The top is a sheet of glass laid over a black lacquered base and framed in a slim aluminium edge, and both pedestals carry the same bright rim. Every drawer has a sculpted aluminium pull that catches the light, and the top drawer on each side has a lock.

Black against silver is a contrast that never gets old. Here it is honest and practical: wooden drawers with black painted fronts, held in a frame that is light to look at yet steady to work at. With a width of 140 cm there is room for a laptop, a lamp and a stack of books, while the open space between the pedestals keeps the desk from feeling heavy.

This desk has lived a working life and shows it. The glass top has scratches and the black lacquer beneath it shows a fine crackled pattern. The painted drawer fronts carry small marks and specks. The wooden drawers had woodworm in the past: the desk has been treated and the woodworm is no longer active. The small holes remain, as part of its story.

Put it in a home office, a studio or a quiet corner of the living room and it brings order without fuss: a desk with a clear purpose that kept its good looks. As with every piece we select, Vintz ships it fully insured in a custom made wooden crate.
